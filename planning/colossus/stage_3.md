# Stage 3 — The Pull

*You are on rails. You may not have noticed when it happened.*

---

## Mode

Full rails. Forward motion is no longer the player's. The Colossus's gravity has them. The player steers left/right/up/down within a corridor — that is all.

---

## What Is Happening

The player cannot stop. Cannot go back. The pull that began in Stage 2 has resolved into something undeniable. The Colossus is ahead. The player is being drawn toward him whether they want to be or not.

All phenomena active: gravitational fields within the rail corridor push the player laterally. Obstacles from the wake become denser and more aggressive — harder debris, stranger geometry. Then, toward the end of the stage, they begin to thin. Nothing stands between the seeker and the Colossus. Even the obstacles lose interest.

---

## Oxygen

Oxygen as a primary resource recedes here. The player is on rails — the path is defined. Ghosts of previous runs may appear along the corridor, offering replenishment, but the survival challenge shifts from resource management to navigation and obstacle avoidance.

---

## Atmosphere

- Color temperature shifting — cold blue beginning to move toward something warmer
- Stars thinning. The sky emptying.
- Audio: the signal is gone. Something else has replaced it — vast, low, not quite sound.
- The Colossus is not yet visible but his presence saturates everything.
