# Stage 4 — The Approach / Arrival

*He is here. He has always been here.*

---

## Stage 4 — The Approach

**Mode:** Rails at full intensity, then dissolving.

Obstacles thin toward nothing. The universe stops resisting. Color temperature completes its shift — deep warm orange, cosmic amber. The signal, the debris, the phenomena — all of it falls away. There is only the path and what is at the end of it.

At some point the vessel falls away too. The player figure continues alone, exposed, moving toward him without protection.

---

## Arrival — The Colossus

**Mode:** All-range. Forward motion stops. He is here.

No combat. No objective stated. The player is free to move in the space around him — orbit, drift, approach. The Colossus breathes (per-vertex displacement, sine waves, never repeating). He is enormous. His scale is such that the player may not immediately understand what they are looking at.

The camera spirals in slowly, inevitably. At a certain distance, the geometry of the Colossus surrounds the camera from the inside. The player is inside something vast.

A hold. Six seconds, maybe more. Nothing to do. Just be inside it.

Then: the selector. No explanation. No score. No ending screen.

---

## What This Means

The ending does not answer:
- Whether the transformation is death or transcendence
- Whether the Colossus is alive or dead
- Whether the seeker succeeded or failed
- Whether the frozen seekers along the way were failures or arrivals of a different kind

The game's job is to make these questions feel vast and okay — not to resolve them. Any implementation that accidentally answers one of them should be reconsidered.

---

## Atmosphere

- Warm light, as if from an ancient sun
- Near silence. Whatever sound exists is structural, interior — the sound of being inside something
- No music, or music that resolves into something unresolved
