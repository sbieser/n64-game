# Stage — Geyser Moon

*For a moment, you are going the wrong way.*

---

## Setting

A small moon with cryovolcanism at its southern pole. A subsurface ocean under pressure from overlying ice and a warm core finds cracks and forces them open. The cracks become geysers: columns of liquid water and ice crystals shooting hundreds of kilometers into space. The moon is building one of its host planet's rings from itself — what the player crosses is the active ejection site, the place where the moon becomes the ring.

From space the geyser plume is visible as a faint shimmer above the pole. Getting close means entering it.

---

## Mode

This stage briefly inverts the direction of travel.

The player enters the geyser plume. The environmental rail here is upward: ice crystals and water vapor ejecting from the moon's surface into space, carrying the player with them. For the central portion of the stage, the player is moving away from the Colossus. Not by choice — by physics.

The plume disperses at altitude. The player emerges above the moon in thin space, the geyser column below them, the journey's direction ahead. The brief reversal makes the correct heading clearer by contrast — the player has been moved wrong and now re-orients back toward what they were seeking.

---

## Threat

No single environmental threat dominates. The geyser jet's core is dangerous if the player drifts into it (ice particle impact, structural stress). The margins are navigable.

The operative challenge is disorientation: holding the correct heading while the environment carries you in the wrong direction. The cosmic signal is behind rather than ahead for the duration of the plume. The player must trust it will return to its correct position when the plume releases them.

---

## The Ghost_Reacher

At the geyser rim — frozen into the ice at the ejection point, at the base of the plume where it begins. Still reaching, arm extended upward, facing the direction the geyser goes: not toward the Colossus, but up and outward into space.

This ghost_reacher breaks the pattern. Every other ghost_reacher faces toward the Colossus. This one faces away — up, following the geyser, which is going in the wrong direction. What does it mean? The player is not told. The ghost_reacher found the source of something and pointed toward it. That source went away from the Colossus. What it was seeking is held open.

---

## Ghost Behavior

Ghost ships inside the plume are carried up and ejected — they do not persist on this stage. The geyser takes them. Only ghosts on the surface approach to the geyser rim, outside the plume, accumulate normally.

**Resource:** oxygen charges. This is still space above the moon's thin atmosphere — normal oxygen replenishment from surface ghosts on the approach. Inside the plume there are no ghosts to find.

---

## Return Memory Trigger

Brief movement in the wrong direction, then re-orientation. On the return journey — already going the right direction — this stage triggers a memory of having been lost briefly, specifically, and finding the way back. Not dramatic disorientation. A small wrong turn. The specific feeling of realizing you've been going wrong and the moment of correcting. The relief that is also slightly funny because it was never actually serious.
