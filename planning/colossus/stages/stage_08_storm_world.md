# Stage 8 — Storm World

*The atmosphere is the obstacle. The atmosphere is also the path.*

---

## Setting

A gas giant's upper atmosphere — or a terrestrial planet with a runaway greenhouse atmosphere and permanent planet-wide storm systems. Winds exceeding hundreds of kilometers per hour. Lightning discharges visible from space. The player enters the cloud layer and is immediately inside the storm.

The storm is not caused by the Colossus. It is simply what this world is. Massive planets generate massive weather. The Colossus passed through this system long before the storm, and the storm will continue long after any seeker passes through. The indifference is total and impersonal. The storm doesn't want you dead. It has no wants at all.

Reference: Jupiter's actual storm bands, Venus's sulfuric acid clouds and 200km/h winds, exoplanet HD 189733b where silicate clouds produce glass rain at 5,400km/h.

---

## Mode

Environmental rails — the storm corridor. The storm's internal structure has lanes of relative calm: the gaps between discharge zones, the margins of active cells. The player rides the storm, steered by wind, finding the navigable channels within the violence.

---

## Threat — Electrical Discharge

Lightning. The storm discharges constantly and without pattern that can be predicted far in advance. Direct hit: hull damage, accumulating.

**Hull integrity** is the tracked resource. It depletes on discharge hits and cannot be fully replenished — only slowed by careful navigation.

This is the only stage with an explicit timing mechanic: observe the discharge rhythm in a zone, find the gap, move through it, stop in the calm behind it.

See `mechanics/oxygen.md` for the planetary equivalent table. See `mechanics/ghosts.md` for discharge shielding charges.

---

## Seeking

The storm is the seeking challenge. The cosmic signal competes with storm EM — audible, but harder to localize cleanly. The primary seeking work here is not toward the Colossus but toward the vortex where the ghost_reacher is.

The eye of a persistent vortex — a stable structure in the storm's geometry — is the destination. The ghost_reacher's signal carries from within it: calmer, less EM noise, the organic quality of the Colossus signal present in the one place where the storm's interference is minimal. The player seeks that clarity. Moving toward calmer signal means moving toward the vortex.

**Watch → Wait for gap → Move → Stop in the calm behind it**

The patience the void asked for expressed as survival rather than listening. The same discipline, different stakes.

See `mechanics/seeking_mechanic.md`.

---

## The Ghost_Reacher

In the calm of a persistent vortex — a stable structure in the storm's geometry, an eye that has been rotating long enough to be considered permanent on a geological timescale. Ancient, as everything in this part of the journey is ancient. The ghost_reacher is in the only calm place in the stage, and has been there long enough that the storm has grown around it like a cathedral.

The player must navigate through the discharge zones to reach the vortex. Once inside the eye, the contrast is complete: violent storm on all sides, absolute calm at center. The ghost_reacher sits in the calm.

---

## Ghost Behavior

Ghost ships persist in calm zones and are destroyed or scattered in active discharge zones. The planet does not reliably remember here — storm geometry shifts between visits. A ghost that was sheltered may be in an active discharge zone next time.

**Resource:** discharge shielding. The ghost's electromagnetic protection systems, still partially active, can absorb hull damage from a subsequent discharge hit.

See `mechanics/ghosts.md`.

## Return: Memory Fragment

*Trigger: being inside while turbulence happens outside*

A corner room. Rain on a drain that didn't work right — a particular sound, a particular delay. A tree outside hitting the roof in its own rhythm. Reading something strange and vast late at night while everyone else was asleep. Nothing happened. But that specific configuration — the sound, the quality of the dark, the feeling of being alone inside something — that is what stays.

See `mechanics/memories.md` for the crystallization moment and accumulation mechanics.

## Atmosphere

**Visual:**

Cloud structures at multiple scales: the large-scale banding of the storm system, the local turbulence of active cells, the gap between them where calmer air exists. Lightning illuminates the cloud structures from within — the clouds flash, the interior of each storm cell briefly visible as a silhouette before the light fails.

The eye of the vortex is the visual contrast: outside, chaos; inside, a column of still air with the ghost_reacher at its center. The cloud walls of the vortex curve upward and outward. Looking up from the eye: open sky, briefly. Stars visible for the first time since entering the atmosphere.

The discharge zones have a charged quality — a blue-white quality to the air that precedes a lightning strike. The player learns to read this as warning.

**Audio:**

The storm is the loudest stage in the game. Wind, EM interference, lightning strikes, the continuous electromagnetic noise of weather. The cosmic signal is barely present in the thick of it — audible in the calmer lanes, overwhelmed in the discharge zones.

The eye: silence. Or near-silence — the signal, clear. The ghost_reacher's signal from within the vortex is the cleanest audio the player has heard since Stage 1's open void. The contrast with the surrounding storm is the stage's emotional center.
