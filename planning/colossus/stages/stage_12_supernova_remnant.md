# Stage — Supernova Remnant

*A star died here. Everything visible is the aftermath of that.*

---

## Setting

The expanding shell of gas and debris from a stellar explosion — filaments of superheated gas, shockwave fronts moving outward at thousands of kilometers per second, the dense collapsed remnant at the center. The explosion happened long enough ago that the shell has expanded to dozens of light-years across. What was a single point of violence is now a vast structured space, still moving, still expanding, still hot.

Everything visible was inside a star. Every particle of gas in this nebula was forged in stellar fusion and expelled in the moment of death. The player is moving through the interior of something that used to be a sun.

The Colossus may have passed through this region after the explosion, or before — when the star was still alive. Whether the Colossus's gravity contributed to the stellar death is unanswerable. Stars die at their own scale and on their own schedule. The Colossus moves through everything on its own scale and schedule. That they overlapped here may mean nothing. It may mean everything.

---

## Mode

Free-roam. The remnant is large enough that the player has genuine navigational freedom — but it is also cluttered, thermally complex, and electromagnetically loud. The complexity is the stage's character.

---

## Seeking — Synthesis

Every sensing modality developed across the journey is active simultaneously here:
- **Audio:** the signal is present but scattered by the dense gas filaments
- **Thermal:** the remnant radiates infrared — but unevenly, the filaments hotter than the gaps
- **Haptic:** gravitational waves from the dense remnant at the center, detectable via rumble pak
- **Visual:** lensing from the dense core, though less clean than the gas giant stage — the intervening gas distorts it

No single channel is sufficient. Each gives partial information. Only reading all of them together yields a reliable heading. This is the stage the whole journey has been building toward mechanically — every sense the player has developed, needed at once.

See `mechanics/seeking_mechanic.md` for the synthesis stage documentation.

---

## The Ghost_Reacher

At the center — near the dense remnant itself, at the closest survivable approach. Ancient beyond reckoning: the star this remnant came from died long before any human civilization. The ghost_reacher has been here in the hot center of the aftermath since the expansion began to cool enough to approach.

The signal from this ghost_reacher is strong — the remnant's density amplifies rather than attenuates it. It is the clearest ghost_reacher signal since Stage 1. After stages of muffled, scattered, attenuated signal, something broadcasts clearly. The player has been getting closer to the Colossus for a long time. Everything here confirms it.

---

## Ghost Behavior

Ghost ships in the remnant have thermal signatures from the surrounding hot gas. They are visually brighter than in the void — the gas illuminates them. The flickering that marks ghost ships is harder to see against the bright filaments; the vent particles remain the reliable tell.

**Resource:** oxygen charges. Standard space-stage mechanic, though the environment makes ghost ships more visually dramatic than anywhere before.

---

## Return Memory Trigger

Aftermath. Something enormous ended here, long ago, and what remains is its expansion outward. On the return journey this space triggers a memory of aftermath — something that ended, the specific quality of the time after. Not grief. Not relief. The particular feeling of being in the after of something, when the event itself is over and what remains is just what it left behind. Small things. Specific things. What was left.
