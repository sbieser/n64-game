# The Colossus

## The Idea

A rails-based 3D space traversal game for real Nintendo 64 hardware. The player travels through deep space on a fixed forward path — obstacle survival, not combat — searching for a mythic cosmic entity. When found, the player becomes one with it.

Emotional register: **meditative, inevitable, cosmic.** Not an action game.

The game asks: *do I dare disturb the universe — lest I become he, the Colossus?*

## What We Know

- The player moves forward automatically; analog stick steers within a bounded play area
- Obstacles exist to be avoided, not destroyed — the universe is indifferent, not hostile
- Death records the player's position; subsequent runs render that ghost there, frozen, still reaching forward
- The Colossus is at the end. What finding him means is intentionally unresolved.
- The ending must never answer whether the transformation is death or transcendence

## What Is Open

- How long is a run? How many distinct zones or stages?
- How strictly three-act? Or something looser?
- What does difficulty look like — obstacle density, gravitational pull, or something else?
- When and how does the Colossus become visible?
- Does the run repeat, or is completion the end?
