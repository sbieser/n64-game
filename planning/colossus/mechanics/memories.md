# Memories

*The return journey's signal. Interior rather than exterior. Personal rather than cosmic. The same seeking loop, transformed.*

---

## What Memories Are

Positional audio fragments of something personal and small — a life before the seeking began. Not music. Not narration. Fragments. Specific sounds that belonged to a specific life.

What the memories are is not stated by the game. The player understands gradually through accumulation. By the time the return journey ends, they carry enough to understand what they left behind. The understanding is theirs, not the game's to give.

---

## The Memory Signal

Mechanically identical to the Colossus's signal on the forward journey:

- **Volume** = proximity to the memory's source direction
- **Pan** = horizontal direction
- **Pitch shift** = vertical direction

The seeking loop is unchanged: stop, rotate, listen, orient, thrust. The tool the player developed across the entire forward journey applies directly. What changes is the character of what they're following.

The Colossus's signal was organic, irregular, almost alive — the plasma wave / chorus phenomenon. Vast. Not intended for the player.

The memory signal is small, specific, warm. Also not intended — memories don't broadcast, they surface. But they have direction, and the direction is home.

---

## Accumulation

Memories do not arrive complete. They accumulate across the return stages — fragments recovered as the player moves back through each space.

Early return stages: a few fragments. Strange in isolation. Not yet legible as a picture of something.

Later return stages: more fragments. Patterns emerging. Something recognizable taking shape.

Final stage (the void): enough to understand. Not everything — the game does not give everything. Enough.

The accumulation mirrors the forward journey's knowledge accumulation: on the way out, the player learned to read space. On the way back, the player reads themselves.

---

## Character

The memory signal should feel unmistakably different from the Colossus's signal without being explained as different. The contrast teaches the player what changed:

- Small where the Colossus's signal was vast
- Warm where it was cold
- Specific where it was ambiguous
- Interior where it was exterior

The ambiguity that protected the Colossus's signal — was it intentional? a warning? an invitation? — does not apply here. These are memories. They were never a broadcast. They are yours.

---

## Relationship to Seeking Mechanic

The memory signal is the interior instance of the seeking mechanic's signal. The full seeking loop documentation, challenge types, and sensory arc all apply on the return journey as the forward journey's challenges reappear in reverse.

The primary difference: noise interference and other environmental challenges on the return may feel different emotionally. Losing the memory signal in stellar noise, for example, carries different weight than losing the Colossus signal. The mechanics are the same. The stakes feel different.
